Science, STEM and Computing Contests for Canadian Students

Science and STEM contests include science fairs, team engineering challenges, and programming competitions. Several are staged, with regional events leading to national ones, so the deadline that matters is often the regional entry date.

6 contests listed, soonest deadline first.

ExploraVision (Toshiba/NSTA)

Toshiba / NSTA

Futuristic STEM challenge for teams of 2–4 in Grades 4–12 from US or Canadian schools; envision technology 20 years ahead.

📅 Register by Jan 27, 2027; project due about Feb 3, 2027 · 📚 Grades 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 · 🏅 Cash

Canadian Computing Competition (CCC)

University of Waterloo – CEMC

Annual programming competition for secondary school students; accessible to beginners and challenges experienced coders.

📅 February 17, 2027 (your school registers earlier) · 📚 Grades 7–9, 10–12 · 🏅 Certificate

Let's Talk Science Challenge

Let's Talk Science

Free team-based STEM competition for Grades 6–8; includes a quiz and engineering challenge held across Canada and online.

📅 Spring 2027 (March–May) · 📚 Grades 4–6, 7–9 · 🏅 Recognition

Canada-Wide Science Fair (CWSF)

Youth Science Canada

Canada's largest youth STEM event for Grades 7–12; students qualify through regional fairs, then compete nationally for major scholarships.

📅 May 2027 (regional fairs precede) · 📚 Grades 7–9, 10–12 · 🏅 Scholarship

Regeneron ISEF (via Youth Science Canada)

Society for Science / Youth Science Canada

World's largest high school science fair; Canadians qualify through CWSF to represent Canada as Team Canada-ISEF.

📅 May 2027 · 📚 Grades 10–12 · 🏅 Cash

International Research Olympiad (IRO)

International Research Olympiad

Global STEM research competition for students aged 13–18 worldwide, testing research and analytical skills beyond the classroom.

📅 Varies (check website) · 📚 Grades 7–9, 10–12 · 🏅 Certificate
